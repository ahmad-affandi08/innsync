### Slice 36 (2026-10-03): employee records, personnel papers, warnings and offboarding

- Status: `TASK-HR-001`, `-002` and `-003` are `REVIEW`; `TASK-HR-005` was `IN_PROGRESS` until slice 37 added the roster and the closing of upcoming shifts. ADR/BR: a person who leaves is never deleted; personnel papers are sensitive (a separate privilege, private files, every opening audited, nothing of their content in the audit trail); human resource and the identity module meet only through the shared contracts `StaffDirectory` and `StaffAccess`.
- Context: new bounded context `HumanResource` (`app/Modules/HumanResource`), migration 88 (`hr_employees`, `hr_documents`, `hr_offboard_items` append-only by trigger, `hr_settings`), `EmployeeStore` with `DatabaseEmployeeStore`, `EmployeeService`, `DocumentService`, `EmployeeController`, page `hr/pages/employees`, routes `/hr/...`. Privileges `hr.employee.view`, `hr.employee.manage` and `hr.document.manage`. In the shared kernel: `StaffDirectory::members` and the new contract `StaffAccess` (`DatabaseStaffAccess` in the identity module).
- **Employees (`HR-001`).** A manager writes a record: number `EMP-000001`, name, department, position, the day joined (at most 90 days ahead), kind of contract (permanent, fixed term, probation, daily, intern) with its end date (required unless permanent), the direct supervisor (an employee still working here; nobody is under themselves, directly or through others), the account they sign in with (one account for one person, someone who works in the property), phone and e-mail. Changes carry the version of the screen and are audited without the contact details.
- **Papers (`HR-002`).** Contract, identity, certificate, medical check or other, each with a title, the day issued and the day it holds until, and its file (PDF, JPG or PNG up to 5 MB, kept privately as sensitive). A newer paper may replace one of the same person; the older stays, marked replaced. Only the privilege for papers sees, adds or opens them, and each adding and each opening is audited with the kind and the employee's number.
- **Warnings (`HR-003`).** For people still working: a contract ending or ended, a paper holding until a date that is near or past, and a paper every employee must have that is missing (baseline: contract and identity; the owner chooses). They are warned of from 30 days before (baseline, 1 to 365, saved with the version of the setting) and listed, the most urgent first, for managers and the papers privilege. There is no push or e-mail yet.
- **Offboarding (`HR-005`).** A manager records why the person leaves (resigned, terminated, contract ended, retired, other; a reason is required for terminated and other), the day (not after today, not before they joined) and what they had to hand back, each marked handed back or not. Someone with people under them must name who supervises these from now on. The person's roles in the property end, so they can no longer sign in to it (their account, and everything that names them, stays); their papers start the retention period of `hr_personnel_document` counted from the day they left; the record, the papers and the hand-back list can no longer be changed (the hand-back list also not by the database).
- Not yet: taking someone back, a hand-back list that is filled in later, a self-service portal (`HR-004`), the dashboard card of warnings, an e-mail of the warnings, ending the person's open work (work orders, tasks) given to them.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/HumanResource/EmployeeHttpTest.php` (numbering, validation of every field, supervisor loops, one account for one person, versions, who may see and write; papers kept privately, replaced and never deleted, openings audited, who may; warnings for ending and ended contracts and papers, missing papers, the warning period and its version; offboarding with supervisor hand-over, the hand-back list, access ended, retention started, the record closed, and the database refusing changes). Seen in the browser: an employee written, a paper added, and the person offboarded.

### Slice 37 (2026-10-03): shifts, the roster and the staffing needs

- Status: `TASK-HR-010`, `-011` and (with the closing of upcoming shifts) `-005` are `REVIEW`. ADR/BR: each planned day keeps the times of the shift as it was planned; a day already past is not planned again here; the roster plans only people still working and within their contract.
- Context: migration 89 (`hr_shift_patterns`, `hr_roster_entries`, `hr_staffing_minimums`), `RosterStore` with `DatabaseRosterStore`, `RosterService`, `RosterController`, page `hr/pages/roster`, privilege `hr.roster.manage` (people with the employee privileges only see the roster). Offboarding calls `RosterService::closeFor`.
- **Shifts (`HR-010`).** The owner configures them: a code (letters and digits), a name, the times it starts and ends (an end before the start means the shift ends the next morning, as a night shift does), an optional second part for a split shift (both parts within one day, one after the other), or a day off with no times. The usual ones for an Indonesian hotel can be written in one step: morning 07:00 to 15:00, afternoon 15:00 to 23:00, night 23:00 to 07:00, split 07:00 to 11:00 and 17:00 to 21:00, and day off. A shift is changed (with the version of the screen) or retired and brought back; its code and whether it is a day off do not change; it is never deleted.
- **Roster (`HR-010`).** A manager plans a person for a shift on a day, or clears the day, for a week or a month (up to 62 days), by department or all; one change may cover several people and days (at most 800 in all). The days copied from the week before fill only empty days, and skip people who have left, days that are past and days outside a contract. A planned day keeps the code, the department, the times and the hours (a split or night shift counted right) it was planned with, so changing a shift later changes none of them.
- **Needs (`HR-011`).** For each department and each worked shift the owner says the fewest people it needs (0 to 200; 0 takes the need away). The roster lists every coming day, from the business date on, that has fewer people planned than that, with how many it has and needs; days off do not count as people on the shift, and people who have left do not count.
- **Offboarding (`HR-005`).** When a person leaves, the days planned for them after the day they left are taken out of the roster; the day count is kept in the audit entry of the offboarding.
- Not yet: swapping shifts (`HR-017`), an assignment of several shifts in one day, a minimum that depends on the day of the week or the occupancy, a published or locked roster.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/HumanResource/RosterHttpTest.php` (the usual shifts, hours of a night and a split shift, every refused input, retiring; planning days, clearing, the times kept when a shift is changed, past days, days before joining and after a contract, retired shifts, the size limits, who may; copying a week into empty days only; the needs and the warnings with their edge cases; offboarding clearing the upcoming days). Seen in the browser: the usual shifts written, and a person planned on a day from the roster.

### Slice 38 (2026-10-03): attendance by phone, the hours against the roster and the staff on duty

- Status: `TASK-HR-012` (by phone; the attendance device at the property is not built), `-013` and `-014` are `REVIEW`. ADR/BR (baselines, all configurable by the owner in the attendance settings): the property's position and a radius (100 m to start with) decide where a person may clock in; the selfie is optional (off to start with); a supervisor may write a day by hand with a reason when the phone cannot be used.
- Context: migration 90 (`hr_attendance_settings`, `hr_attendance`), `AttendanceStore` with `DatabaseAttendanceStore`, `AttendanceService`, `AttendanceController`, contract `StaffOnDuty` (read by the dashboard), page `hr/pages/attendance`, privilege `hr.attendance.manage`, retention category `hr_attendance_photo` (90 days, at most 365, counted from the time recorded; added to `config/retention.php` and the compliance baseline).
- **Clocking (`HR-012`).** A person clocks in and out for their own account only, for a day they are planned on the roster, from 3 hours before the shift starts to 4 hours after it ends. The phone sends its position; the server measures the distance to the property and refuses beyond the radius. Only the distance is stored, never the coordinates of the person. When the selfie is required, a photo is stored privately (sensitive file, opened only by people with the attendance privilege) and expires with the retention category. A day is clocked in once and out once; a repeated tap with the same key does nothing twice.
- **Manual entry (`HR-012`).** A supervisor with `hr.attendance.manage` writes the in and out time of a day up to 7 days back, for a day planned on the roster, with a reason; the entry says who wrote it and why, and is audited. A row of attendance is never deleted (database trigger).
- **Hours against the roster (`HR-013`).** Computed when read, from the roster (the times kept when it was planned) and the records: late minutes after a grace (10 minutes), early leave after a grace (10 minutes), extra minutes after the shift ends (counted only past 30 minutes), and absent (a planned day that has ended with no clock-in). Status of a day: upcoming, not in yet, on duty, present, missing out, absent. Nothing is stored that could differ from the roster, so a correction of either shows at once. A day by period (up to 62 days) is summed per person.
- **Staff on duty (`HR-014`).** The dashboard has a card of the people on duty now, per department and shift, from the roster and the clock-ins; it is shown to people who have an HR privilege, can be hidden or moved like the other cards (`staff` joins the preference keys) and links to the attendance page.
- Not yet: the attendance device at the property (`HR-012`), payroll that uses these hours.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/HumanResource/AttendanceHttpTest.php` (clock in and out inside and outside the radius, the window, once a day, who may; the selfie required and kept privately; manual entry with its limits and audit; late, early, extra and absent on a day and on a period; the staff on duty on the dashboard card). `DashboardPreferenceTest` follows the new card. Not seen in a browser this slice; the page type-checks and builds.

### Slice 39 (2026-10-03): overtime approved beforehand and corrections of attendance

- Status: `TASK-HR-018` and `-019` are `REVIEW`. ADR/BR (baselines the owner can change): overtime counts as approved only when it was approved before the planned end of the shift; a correction of attendance is never made without an approval (mandatory subject: with no policy it is refused); a day may be corrected up to 90 days back.
- Context: migration 91 (`hr_overtime`, `hr_attendance_corrections`), `OvertimeStore` and `AttendanceCorrectionStore` with their `Database*` classes, `OvertimeService`, `AttendanceCorrectionService`, `ShiftTimes` (where a planned day starts and ends, shared with attendance), `AttendanceAdjustmentController`, tabs Overtime and Corrections on `hr/pages/attendance`, approval subjects `hr.overtime` (optional) and `hr.attendance-correction` (mandatory) in `config/approvals.php`, privilege `hr.attendance.manage` for both.
- **Overtime (`HR-018`).** A supervisor asks for 15 to 480 minutes after the planned end of a shift, for one person on a planned working day from yesterday to 31 days ahead, with a reason, and only while that shift is not over. One request for a person and day is open or approved at a time. With no policy for `hr.overtime` the request is approved when made; with one, it waits for the chain and the person who asked takes the decision (approved, or rejected). A request can be cancelled until the shift ends; it is never deleted (database trigger).
- **Approved against not approved (`HR-018`).** Worked out whenever attendance is read: the time past the end of the shift is overtime up to the minutes approved, but only if they were approved before the shift ended; the rest of the extra time (past the "extra hours count after" setting) is shown as extra time nobody approved. Day rows, the period summary and the person's own shift show approved overtime, not approved extra time, and the minutes granted.
- **Corrections (`HR-019`).** A supervisor asks to change the clock-in and clock-out of a person on a planned day up to 90 days back (also a day with no record), with a reason. It opens an approval bound to the times before and after. Until the approvers decide nothing changes; then the person who asked applies it: rejected closes it, approved changes the record, but only if the record still says what it said when it was asked for (else the correction must be asked for again). The side of the record that changed becomes a manual record with the reason; the distance of a changed side is dropped. One correction waits for each person and day. The times before and after, the reason, the approval, who asked and who applied are kept; the values never change and a correction is never deleted (database triggers). The audit entry holds the times before and after.
- **Recalculation (`HR-019`).** Lateness, early leaving, extra time and overtime follow at once because they are worked out on read; the event `hr.attendance.corrected` goes out with the person and day for whatever is later built on the hours (payroll).
- Not yet: overtime on a day off or before a shift, a rate or a pay value for overtime (payroll), overtime or a correction asked for by the employee in the portal (`HR-004`), a period that is closed so that corrections need a different chain, a notice to the approvers (needs a notification channel).
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/HumanResource/AttendanceAdjustmentHttpTest.php` (approved, not approved and late-approved overtime on a day, the summary and the person's row; every refused input, one open request a day, cancel before and after the shift ends, who may; the chain with approved, rejected and late decisions; a correction with no policy, every refused input, waiting, who applies, applied with the day recalculated, the audit and the event, the triggers; a correction rejected, cancelled, overtaken by a change of the record, and a day with no record filled). Seen in the browser: overtime asked for, a correction asked for, approved by another person and applied, and the day showing the corrected times.

### Slice 40 (2026-10-03): leave, sick leave and permits with a yearly balance

- Status: `TASK-HR-015` and `-016` are `REVIEW`. ADR/BR (baselines the owner can change): annual leave is 12 days a year once 12 months of work have passed; sick leave needs a paper from the third day; a permit has no pay; maternity leave is not taken from a balance and needs a paper; leave that comes out of the balance starts today or later, other leave may start up to 7 days back; every request goes through the approval chain (mandatory subject `hr.leave`: with no policy no request is taken).
- Context: migration 92 (`hr_leave_types`, `hr_leave_requests`, `hr_leave_days`, `hr_leave_adjustments`), `LeaveStore` with `DatabaseLeaveStore`, `LeaveTypeService`, `LeaveService`, `LeaveController`, page `hr/pages/leave`, privilege `hr.leave.manage`, the roster showing leave cells, and the offboarding closing leave. Evidence is kept as a private sensitive file in the personnel papers purpose, so its retention starts with the offboarding.
- **Kinds (`HR-016`).** The owner writes them: code, name, whether the kind comes out of a yearly balance and how many days a year, how many months of work come first, from how many days a paper is needed (empty never, 0 always), and whether it is paid. The usual four are written in one step. A kind is changed with the version of the screen (the code and whether it has a balance never change) or retired and brought back; requests keep the kind as it was when they were made.
- **Request (`HR-015`).** A person asks for themselves with their own account; the person who manages leave may ask for someone else. First and last day (at most 120 days; from the balance, within one calendar year and not before the right begins), a reason, and a paper (PDF, JPG or PNG up to 3 MB) that is required when the days are more than the kind says. Refused when the person has leave waiting or approved in those days, or attendance recorded in them, or when the balance is too small. The days counted are the calendar days less the days the roster shows as a day off.
- **Decision (`HR-015`).** The chain the owner configured decides; the person who asked takes the decision once it is made (rejected closes it). Approving takes the days out of the roster (each planned shift is kept in the leave day to put back), so staffing warnings show the gap and nobody is marked absent; the days can no longer be planned, copied or assigned. The event `hr.leave.approved` goes out for payroll. A waiting request is cancelled by the person who asked or who manages leave; an approved one only before it starts, and its shifts go back into the roster. Offboarding cancels the waiting and the future leave and starts the retention of the papers.
- **Balance (`HR-016`).** For a year (last, this, next): right (the kind's days once the months of work are over) + adjustments − days taken (approved) − days waiting for approval. The person sees their own; the manager sees everyone and adjusts with a reason (a number of days, more or less, never below zero), which is audited and never changed or deleted (database triggers).
- Not yet: carrying unused days over automatically or letting them lapse (adjust by hand), half days, a right that grows in proportion to the months worked, public holidays and collective leave, leave that covers two years, the supervisor deciding for their team without the chain, the leave pay in payroll, a notice to the approvers (needs a notification channel), the person's own portal (`HR-004`).
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/HumanResource/LeaveHttpTest.php` (the kinds with their checks, versions and retiring; annual leave with no policy, every refused input, the balance held while waiting, the decision, the days leaving the roster and not planned again, cancelling before the start, rejecting; sick leave with and without a paper, who may open it and the audit; leave on behalf of another and attendance blocking it; adjustments and their limits and triggers; offboarding). `RosterHttpTest` and `EmployeeHttpTest` still pass. Seen in the browser: a request made with the balance held, approved by another person, taken, and the roster showing the leave.

### Slice 49 (2026-10-03): the payroll run — calculation, approval, hand-over to Finance, adjustments and reopening

- **HR-031** — `PayrollCalculator` (pure, whole minor units, half-up shares) works out each person's month from `PayrollRunService::calculate`: earnings by kind (whole, by days present of days planned, or for each day present; at the pay in force on the last day of the month), overtime (approved only, 1/173 of the wage of basic pay and fixed allowance an hour, ×1.5 the first hour of a day and ×2 after), absence and unpaid leave (1/25 of the wage a day), lateness (per minute, 0 until set), BPJS Kesehatan/JHT/JP for the person and the employer (and JKK/JKM for the employer) up to the ceilings and only for the schemes the person is in, and the income tax by the annualised method (income plus what the employer pays toward health, accident and death cover, less the cost of the job and the person's own JHT/JP, times twelve, less the PTKP of the status, through the brackets, over twelve, with the surcharge without a tax number). Employees with nothing set are not in the run and the screen lists them; a person without tax data is calculated with the usual status and the line says so, as it does when nothing was planned in the roster or the deductions exceed the pay. The parameters used are kept with the run. `AttendanceService::periodSummary` now splits overtime into the first hour of each day and the rest.
- **HR-037** — `hr_payroll_runs` one per month with the lifecycle draft → calculated → reviewed → approved → paid → locked (paid and locked follow in Finance). Approval is the mandatory subject `hr.payroll-run` (band = net pay): with no policy the run cannot be approved; the person who asked takes the decision (approved, or the refusal clears the request to ask again). Approving consumes the approval, freezes the lines (triggers refuse insert/update/delete of the lines of an approved, paid or locked run, and the deletion of a run past the draft) and hands the run to Finance through the `PayrollDisbursements` contract (`finance_payroll_disbursements`, status awaiting) and the event `hr.payroll.approved`.
- **HR-038** — Nothing approved is overwritten. A correction is an adjustment (`hr_payroll_adjustments`, plus or minus, taxable or not, with the run it corrects and a reason; never deleted, cancelled while open) that the next run for a later month takes in as its own line; calculating again gives the adjustments back and takes them in once. An approved run that Finance has not paid can be reopened with a reason by a person holding `hr.payroll.reopen`: what it was is kept in the append-only `hr_payroll_revisions`, Finance gets the run back (withdrawn; refused when it was paid), the run returns to calculated and `hr.payroll.reopened` goes out; after payment only an adjustment is possible.
- Screens: `/hr/payroll/runs` (the run and its steps, the pay of each person with a breakdown, adjustments). Not yet: Finance verifying and paying the run, marking it paid and locking it, the payslips (`HR-034`), the service charge (`HR-032/033`), the export (`HR-035`), proration of a raise or a joiner in the middle of a month, the monthly TER tax tables.

### Slice 50 (2026-10-03): Finance pays the run, the run is locked, payslips and the payroll file

- **HR-037 (completed)** — Finance gets every approved run on `/finance/payroll` (`finance_payroll_disbursements`): a person with `finance.payroll.verify` confirms the net pay by typing it (a different amount is refused), and a different person with `finance.payroll.pay` pays it with the method (transfer or cash) and a reference; the tax and the social security owed on top are shown beside the net pay. Paying publishes `finance.payroll.paid`; `PayrollPaidConsumer` (`hr.payroll-paid`, idempotent) marks the run paid with its reference, and a person with the payroll right locks it (`POST /hr/payroll/runs/{id}/lock`). Only an approved run goes to Finance, so only an approved period is ever paid. A run Finance already paid cannot be reopened (the withdrawal is refused).
- **HR-034** — The electronic payslip: `/hr/payslips` lists a person's own payslips of the months that were paid and `/hr/payslips/{run}` shows one (earnings, deductions, what the employer paid, net pay, tax status, days; printable). Written to the audit trail.

### Slice 51 (2026-10-03): the service charge — collection, points, simulation and approval

- **HR-032** — `ServiceChargeCollected` (Finance contract) gives what the revenue days of a month booked as service charge from the rooms and the outlets, by outlet. `ServiceChargeSettingsService` keeps the property's policy: the staff's share of it (baseline 100%), the reserve kept back for damage and loss (baseline 5%), the points of each position (matched on the position written on the employee record, without regard to case) and the points of a position without any (baseline 1); the screen says when the usual values still apply and lists the positions that have no points. `ServiceChargeService::calculate` takes the collected amount, keeps the staff's share, sets aside the reserve and shares the rest with `ServiceChargeCalculator`: a person's weight is their points times the whole percent of their planned days they were present (paid leave counts as being there), each share is rounded down, and the few units rounding leaves are the residue kept with the reserve; the arithmetic does not overflow for large amounts.

### Slice 53 (2026-10-03): warnings and awards, notices and policies

- **HR-023** — `ConductService` (`hr_conduct_records`, `/hr/conduct`, permission `hr.conduct.manage`, sensitive): a spoken reprimand, a first, second or third warning letter (SP1–SP3) or an award of a person, with the day issued (today or at most 60 days back), the reason, the letter as a private file (PDF or photo, at most 3 MB, sensitive) and, for a warning, the day until which it holds — by default 3 months for a reprimand and 6 for a warning letter, which is also the longest a warning letter may hold; an award never lapses. A warning shows as holding until its day passes, then as expired. Nothing is deleted: a record made by mistake is revoked with a reason and stays on file (triggers refuse deletion). The person sees their own records and opens their own letter; people with the right see everyone's and every letter opened is audited. When a person leaves, the retention of their letters starts with that of their papers. `hr.conduct.recorded` goes out; the employee's own page tells them how many warnings still hold.
- **HR-024** — `AnnouncementService` (`hr_announcements`, `hr_announcement_reads`, `/hr/announcements`, permission `hr.announcement.manage`): a notice or a policy for everyone or one department, with a text, an optional document and an optional last day. The people with the right see, for each publication, how many of its audience read and confirmed it and the names of those who have not. What is published is not edited: it is withdrawn with a reason and a new one is published, so who read what stays true. The employee's page warns of what waits for their confirmation.
