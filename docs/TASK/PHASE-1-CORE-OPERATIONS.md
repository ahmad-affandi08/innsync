# Phase 1 — Core Operations (Months 1–4)

Primary scope from PRD: master data/users, Front Office, Housekeeping, Laundry, room board, early Dashboard, guest/room-revenue reporting.

## Mandatory implementation order

1. Foundation + identity/property configuration.
2. Room/type/rate master and business date.
3. Reservation/availability and room state dimensions.
4. Check-in/stay/folio/deposit/payment foundation.
5. Housekeeping assignment/status/inspection.
6. Guest laundry and folio posting.
7. Night audit and daily controls.
8. Dashboard read models and Front Office reports.
9. Phase UAT, migration dry run, cutover rehearsal.

Gate: all Wajib FRs selected for the phase pass UAT and end-to-end reservation → check-in → ancillary charge → payment → checkout → night audit reconciles.

### Step 1–2 groundwork: property configuration, money types, business date (2026-10-01)

- Status: groundwork for `TASK-FO-007` (inventory per room type) and `TASK-FO-008` (rate plans). Neither task is DONE: availability, overbooking and rate plans are not built yet. This entry changes no FR status.
- Traceability: `BR-001`, `BR-002`, `BR-003`, `BR-006`, `BR-008`, `NFR-16`, `NFR-19`, `NFR-26`, `ADR-0006`; `docs/ARCHITECTURE/03` (Property Configuration context).
- Money (`App\Shared\Domain\Money`): `Money` (integer minor units, exact arithmetic, overflow refused, mixed currencies refused, largest-remainder `allocate` for split bills), `Percentage` (basis points parsed from text without floats), `RoundingRule` (increment and mode; half-up, half-even, down, up; symmetric around zero so a reversal is the exact negative), `ChargeScheme` and `ChargeBreakdown` (service charge then regional tax, tax on base only or on base plus service charge, "++" or nett prices; the parts always add up to the total, a nett price must be a whole rounding unit). 29 unit tests, including a seeded 3000-case property test over rates, modes, increments and both price styles.
- Property settings (`property_settings`, `PropertySettingsService`): standard check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00, earliest night audit 23:00, whole-rupiah half-up rounding, 365-day availability horizon, all changeable with a reason, audit entry and optimistic locking; the business date is null until go-live, is set once by an authorized person, and afterwards a database trigger allows it only to move forward (never back, never cleared). `BusinessDateProvider` is the port other contexts use; nothing derives it from the clock. Changing settings needs a recent password confirmation.
- Room master (`room_types`, `rooms`, `RoomCatalogService`): types with a unique code and occupancy, rooms with a unique number per property, never deleted (triggers) but deactivated; a type with active rooms cannot be deactivated, an inactive type cannot receive rooms; every change needs `property.catalog.manage` and a reason and is audited; viewers need `property.catalog.view`; `RoomCatalogReader` is the read-only port for other contexts. Property scope and cross-property type use are refused.
- UI: `/property/rooms` (types and rooms, create, edit, activate, deactivate with consequence dialog and reason) and `/property/settings` (times, rounding, horizon, one-time business date), English and Indonesian, standard error and conflict states, and a generic `useServerAction` hook plus `/reconfirm` (same-site return only) for a lapsed password confirmation.
- Automated evidence: PHPUnit 435 tests (1 skipped, pre-existing) and `npm test` 87 tests pass; new `PropertyConfigurationTest` has 15 MySQL feature tests (permissions, validation with field errors, duplicates, cross-property isolation, deactivation rules, stale edits as 409, undeletable rows, defaults, business date once and forward-only at the database, reconfirm redirect safety). Browser evidence in real Chromium against MySQL: empty states, validation, creating a type and a room, a duplicate number refused inline, deactivation refused while a room is active, setting the business date, saving settings, the Indonesian UI; no unexpected console errors (the 4xx responses are the refusals under test).
- Not included: night audit (`TASK-FO-028`), tax and service-charge configuration per outlet with effective dates (comes with the first charge posting), room status dimensions (Housekeeping/Front Office), and moving a room to another type while reservations exist (decided with reservations).

### TASK-FO-008 evidence — rate plans, prices, restrictions, service charge and tax (2026-10-01)

- Status: `REVIEW`, not `DONE`. Everything the requirement lists is implemented and verified, except one claim that can only be proven once reservations exist: "without changing the history of old reservations" is guaranteed on the rate side (prices are versioned and never edited) and by design on the reservation side (a reservation will snapshot the nightly prices it was given, `TASK-FO-003` and `TASK-FO-009`), but there is no reservation yet to demonstrate it. Package components beyond a free-text inclusions field (for example a priced breakfast line) are not modelled.
- Traceability: `FR-FO-008`, `BR-002`, `BR-003`, `NFR-19`, `ADR-0006`; `docs/PRD` 8.1; Property Configuration context (`docs/ARCHITECTURE/03`).
- Rate plans (`RatePlanService`): code, name, kind (public, corporate, package, OTA, promotion), inclusions text, and whether the quoted price already includes service charge and tax ("nett") or not ("++"); activate and deactivate with optimistic locking. Complimentary stays a folio attribute, not a plan kind (`BR-008`).
- Prices: a nightly price per room type for a date range and chosen weekdays (seasonal and weekend rates are just periods). A period is never edited: repricing or removing supersedes it and keeps the row, and database triggers allow nothing but a one-time supersede and refuse deletes. Overlapping prices for the same plan, room type, dates and weekdays are refused (the plan is locked during a change so two people cannot both add one). Every change needs a reason and is audited.
- Restrictions: minimum and maximum stay, closed to arrival, closed to departure and stop sell per date range, for one room type or all; strictest of each kind wins; versioned the same way.
- Service charge and tax (`ChargeSchemeService`, PRD `Q-05`): effective-dated, append-only schemes for the `rooms` scope with exact basis-point rates and the "tax on base plus service charge" switch; after go-live a scheme cannot start before the business date, so a posted day is never recalculated; rates are property data and nothing is assumed (no scheme means no quote).
- Quote (`RateQuoteService`, port `RateQuoter` for Front Office): prices a stay night by night with the scheme in force on each night and the property's rounding, applies restrictions, and **fails closed**: a missing price, an unconfigured scheme, a stopped or closed date, a minimum or maximum stay, an inactive plan or room type, or a nett price that is not a whole rounding unit each become a named violation instead of a guess. The screen shows the reason in the person's language.
- UI (`/property/rates`, `/property/tax`, shared navigation): plans, prices with weekday picker, restrictions, price check with a night-by-night breakdown, and the service-charge and tax history, in English and Indonesian. Typed amounts are digits only: a value such as `1.500.000` is refused rather than guessed (`parseMajorToMinor`, 6 tests), because "1.500" means different things in the two languages. Price and tax changes need a recent password confirmation; viewing and quoting need `property.rates.view` or `.manage`.
- Also fixed: a screen opened by someone without permission returned a 500 page for an expected refusal; HTML requests now get the framework's 403 or 404 page and JSON keeps the standard envelope.
- Automated evidence: PHPUnit 452 tests (1 skipped, pre-existing), `npm test` 89 tests, Pint, strict TypeScript and build pass. New: 13 MySQL integration tests (`RatePlanTest`: unique codes and permission, overlap rules across weekdays, room types and dates, bad input, repricing history and audit, the database refusing edits, deletes and a second supersede, "++" and nett quotes with weekday prices, the scheme in force per night, missing price and scheme, every restriction type with strictest-wins, inactive plan and stale version, scheme append-only and no back-dating and permission and bad rates, property scope); 4 HTTP tests (`RatePlanHttpTest`); money parsing and the whole-rupiah display. Mutation checks confirmed the tests fail when the strictest minimum stay is not used and when overlap checking is removed. Browser evidence (real Chromium, MySQL): tax scheme, plan, a grouped amount refused, a price shown as whole rupiah, a holiday minimum stay, a bookable quote with the expected 10 percent service charge and 10 percent tax on the sum, a restricted stay explained, the Indonesian UI.
- Not included: package pricing components, per-person or length-of-stay pricing, derived or linked rates, rate approval above a discount threshold (`TASK-FO-013`), and the policy snapshot (`TASK-FO-009`).
