### Slice 57 (2026-10-03): regional tax and service charge

- **FIN-020, -025** — The tax is worked out when a charge is posted, per outlet, from the scheme of the scope of the outlet (`rooms` or an outlet's scope) in force on the business date: service charge and tax rates and whether the tax is charged on the service charge, each scheme with the day it starts from, append-only (a change is a new scheme, never an edit; the database refuses edits). Every posting keeps the rates and the rounding it was made with, so a change never touches what was posted before. `/finance/tax` shows the rates in force for each scope with their history, and the rounding rule of the property.
- **FIN-021** — `TaxService` (`/finance/tax`, rights `finance.tax.view` and `finance.tax.manage`): for each of the last twelve months and each outlet, the base the tax was charged on, the service charge, the tax and the total, from the revenue Finance booked; each month has a deadline (a day of the next month the owner sets, 1 to 28, the 15th until then) and a status: collecting, to be reported, reported, deposited, and overdue when the deadline passed without a deposit. Recording a report (after the month is over) keeps the amounts the books showed that day; recording the deposit keeps the day, the amount and a reference and shows the difference to the books. These records are never changed or deleted (triggers); revenue booked after a month was reported shows as the books' figure beside what was reported. Audited; `finance.tax.reported` goes out.
- **FIN-022** — the service charge collected shows by month and outlet beside the tax, and is what Human Resource shares among the staff (`ServiceChargeCollected`).
- **FIN-023** — `GET /finance/tax/{month}/recap` is a spreadsheet-safe CSV of the month: the taxed revenue by outlet with the service charge, the tax and the total, a total line, and the part kept apart; each file taken is audited.
- **FIN-024** — Kept apart from the taxed revenue, month by month: revenue booked without tax (a base with no tax), complimentary items, other discounts, lines voided, bills cancelled, and charges reversed, so the tax base can be explained.
- Not yet: the monthly rules of a particular region (rates by outlet type are the scopes; the filing format of a given office), corrections of a month that was already reported (shown as a difference), rounding rules that change by date (the rounding is a property setting snapshotted on every posting).

### Slice 62 (2026-10-03): card and QRIS settlements against the books and the bank

- Status: `TASK-FIN-004` is `REVIEW`; `TASK-FIN-037` stays `IN_PROGRESS` (the provider/EDC and bank record are now entered by hand per settlement; they are not read from a file or an API).
- Context: migration 108 (`fin_settlements`, append-only), `SettlementStore`/`DatabaseSettlementStore`, `SettlementService`, `SettlementController`, page `finance/pages/settlements.tsx`; it reuses the exception list of `FR-FIN-019` and the booked `fin_payment_lines` of `FR-FIN-001`.
- **Recording.** Finance (`finance.reconcile.manage`) records each settlement a provider or acquirer made: method (QRIS or card), provider, the stretch of business days it covers (1–31), the date credited, the gross the provider reports, the fee it kept, the net that reached the bank and the bank reference (recorded once). Every day covered must be booked (after its night audit) so the books' figure is final, and a day is covered by one settlement of a provider only.
- **Two comparisons.** The gross against what the books hold as received by that method in those days (received less paid back): a difference means a payment one side does not have. The credit against the gross less the fee: a difference means the bank did not receive what the provider reported. Either difference raises a `settlement_discrepancy` exception dated the last covered day (the amount of the gross difference, else of the credit difference), reconciled by someone other than the person who recorded it, and holds the day from being verified like any open exception. The fee is shown as a share of the gross and flagged above the usual (QRIS 1%, card 3%, baselines to be set by the owner's contract).
- **What is pending.** The page lists the booked days of the last 45 days that received by card or QRIS and are not covered by a settlement, so the person sees what to expect and what is late.
- Not yet: importing a provider or bank statement file, matching individual payments to the provider's lines, fee rates per provider, and chargebacks netted in a settlement (they are raised as their own exceptions).
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Finance/SettlementHttpTest.php` (a matching settlement, differences of the gross and of the credit raising exceptions of the last day with the right amount, a high fee, overlap, unbooked days and duplicate references, every validation, append-only triggers, rights).

### Slice 11 (2026-10-03): accounts payable, the first part of the Finance context

- Status: `TASK-FIN-010`, `-011`, `-012`, `-013` and `-018` are `REVIEW`. `TASK-FIN-006` is `IN_PROGRESS`: payables carry the posting facts, but the other postings (revenue, cash) do not exist yet. NFR/BR: BR-003 (a payable and a paid payment are never changed), BR-004 (no self-approval), BR-005 (idempotent), BR-002 (integer money), BR-006 (document numbers `PAY-nnnnnn`).
- Context: the new bounded context `Finance` (`app/Modules/Finance`), migration 62 (`finance_expense_accounts`, `ap_payables`, `ap_credits`, `ap_credit_applications`, `ap_payments`, `ap_payment_proofs`), screens under `/finance` (Payables, Payments, Due schedule, Aging, Expense accounts) and a Finance entry in the module navigation.
- **Finance reads events, not tables.** `PurchasingPayableConsumer` is an outbox consumer (registered in `config/outbox.php`) for `purchasing.invoice.recognised` and `purchasing.return.posted`. A recognised supplier invoice becomes a payable and a return that came with a supplier credit note becomes a credit; each is made once per source document however often the event is delivered. The payload of those two events carries what Finance needs (supplier code and name, invoice number and date, due date, business date, currency). Because the outbox is processed by the worker, a payable appears when the worker has run, not in the same request.
- **Posting facts (FR-FIN-006)**: a payable keeps the source document, the business date, the event time, the actor and the correlation id of the event that made it. The amount, the dates and the source never change (triggers); only the expense account it is classified under can.
- Choices recorded as configurable baselines:
  - **Balance**: amount less paid payments less credits applied. Status open, partly paid or paid; overdue when something is owed after the due date. A payment that waits for approval counts against what can still be paid.
  - **Payments (FR-FIN-013)**: against one payable, in full or in part, never more than is owed; methods transfer, cash, giro, other; a transfer or a giro needs its reference; the date cannot be in the future; a proof of payment (PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to five) is added afterwards. A paid payment never changes.
  - **Maker-checker (FR-FIN-018)**: approval subject `finance.supplier-payment`, with the threshold and the chain set by the owner in the approval policy (no policy for an amount means it is paid when recorded). A different person approves; the person who recorded the payment takes the decision to release it; a rejected payment is closed with the reason and frees its amount; a pending one can be cancelled by its maker.
  - **Supplier credits**: a credit from a return with a credit note (goods and the tax it credits) is set against payables of the same supplier in parts, never more than the credit has left or the payable still owes.
  - **Aging (FR-FIN-012)**: what is owed at the end of a chosen business date by days past due (not yet due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, over 90), per supplier; payments and credits count from their own date, so an earlier date is stable. The due schedule lists what is overdue and what falls due in the next 7 to 90 days. The dashboard warns of payables past due and payables due in the next seven days.
  - **Expense accounts (FR-FIN-010)**: code (fixed), name, department and category (a fixed baseline list); payables are classified under an active account.
- Known limit: cancelling a payment that waits for approval closes the payment but leaves its approval request pending in the approvers' inbox (the approval gate has no cancel for a request yet); approving it later changes nothing.
- Not yet: payments by batch, reversal of a paid payment (needs a reversal document), recurring expenses, petty cash, accounts receivable, revenue postings and bank reconciliation (the rest of Finance).
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Finance/AccountsPayableHttpTest.php` (18 tests: the event pipeline and its idempotency, partial and full payments, the second approver, credits, aging at the bucket edges, the due schedule, the dashboard alerts, immutability triggers, privileges) and the browser run of the screens in both languages.

### Slice 13 (2026-10-03): accounts receivable

- Status: `TASK-FIN-014` is `REVIEW`. NFR/BR: BR-003 (a receivable, a receipt and a collection note are never changed), BR-005 (idempotent: a folio is billed once, a receipt is posted on the folio once), BR-002 (integer money), BR-006 (document numbers `AR-nnnnnn` and `RCP-nnnnnn`).
- Context: migration 64 (`fin_customers`, `ar_receivables`, `ar_receipts`, `ar_notes`), `CompanyReceivableConsumer`, `CustomerService`, `ReceivableService`, screens `/finance/receivables`, `/finance/receivables/{id}`, `/finance/receivables/aging` and `/finance/customers`; privileges `finance.receivable.view`, `finance.receivable.manage` (customers, manual receivables, collection notes) and `finance.receipt.record` (receipts).
- **From the front office to finance by events.** When a guest of a company or a travel agent checks out and the company's folio still holds a balance, the stay publishes `frontoffice.company_folio.billable` (folio, reservation, guest, company, balance, business date). Finance books one receivable per folio, due after the customer's payment terms (baseline 30 days, changed per customer; a change applies to receivables made afterwards). The customer is made on the first billed folio of a company and keeps the company's link.
- **Customers**: a company or an agent comes from the front office; an online channel or anyone else is added by finance (code, name, kind, terms 0 to 180 days). The code, kind and company never change; a customer that was used is deactivated, not removed.
- **Manual receivables**: finance can make a receivable for an online channel's payout or a function (description, reference, amount, issue date not in the future, optional due date; otherwise issue date plus the customer's terms).
- **Receipts**: in full or in part, never more than is owed; by transfer, giro or online with the reference (no cash here: cash is taken at the cashier and received through the shift); not dated before the receivable or in the future.
- **The folio is settled by the receipt.** A receipt against a receivable that came from a company folio publishes `finance.receivable.received`; the front office consumer (`CompanyReceiptConsumer`) posts a settlement payment (`AR_RECEIPT`, source `ar_receipt`) on the folio once per receipt and closes the folio when nothing is owed. Because the receipt is posted without a cashier shift it does not touch a shift's cash; it counts in the payments of the day it was posted. If the front office took the money first, nothing is posted again and the skipped receipt is audited (`folio.receipt.skipped`). So the company's credit-limit use falls as the money arrives.
- **Collection (penagihan)**: notes of kind reminder, call, promise (with the promised date and optionally the amount), dispute or note, written once and shown newest first; the receivables list shows the promised date and how many days overdue, and filtered to overdue it is the collection worklist, longest overdue first. Notes cannot be added once a receivable is paid.
- **Aging**: what customers owe at the end of a chosen business date by days past due (not yet due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, over 90), per customer; receipts count from their own date, so an earlier date is stable. The dashboard warns of receivables past their due date.
- Not yet: write-off and credit notes (a write-off must also correct the folio, so it needs a reversal document), invoices as printed documents, automatic reminders, a company statement across several folios, and online channels' commission netting.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Finance/AccountsReceivableHttpTest.php` (the event and its idempotency, customers and terms, manual receivables, receipts, notes, aging at the bucket edges and as of an earlier date, filters, privileges, the dashboard alert, immutability) and `tests/Integration/Finance/ReceivableFromCompanyFolioTest.php` (a real checkout bills the company folio; partial and full receipts settle and close it; money the front office already took is not posted twice).

### Slice 14 (2026-10-03): petty cash

- Status: `TASK-FIN-015` is `REVIEW`. NFR/BR: BR-003 (vouchers, ledger entries, proofs and decisions are never changed), BR-004 (a settlement is decided, and a voucher voided, by someone other than the person who made it), BR-005 (idempotent), BR-002 (integer money), BR-006 (document numbers `PCV-nnnnnn` for vouchers and `PCS-nnnnnn` for settlements).
- Context: migration 65 (`fin_petty_funds`, `fin_petty_entries`, `fin_petty_vouchers`, `fin_petty_proofs`, `fin_petty_voids`, `fin_petty_settlements`, `fin_petty_settlement_vouchers`), `PettyCashService`, screens `/finance/petty`, `/finance/petty/{id}` and `/finance/petty/settlements/{id}`; privileges `finance.petty.view` (read), `finance.petty.operate` (the custodian records vouchers, adds proofs and submits the settlement) and `finance.petty.manage` (create and change funds, void vouchers, decide settlements).
- **Imprest system.** A fund is a fixed amount handed to a custodian (a person holding `finance.petty.operate`); the amount and the code never change. The ledger starts with the cash handed over and its balance is the sum of its entries; the database refuses an entry that would take it below zero. A custodian sees only their own funds; viewers and managers see all.
- **Vouchers (pengeluaran)**: recorded only by the custodian, against an active expense account, never beyond the balance or the limit of one voucher (a configurable baseline of IDR 1,000,000 per fund, changeable or cleared by the manager), not dated in the future. A receipt is added as a proof (PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to five) or, when the shop gave none, the reason there is no receipt is written on the voucher. A voucher is voided with a reason by someone other than its maker, and only before it is in a settlement; the money goes back to the fund.
- **Settlement (pertanggungjawaban)**: the custodian submits all vouchers since the last settlement with the cash counted; every voucher needs a proof or a reason, and the count cannot be more than the fund holds when full. A difference between the cash counted and the book balance needs a reason. While a settlement waits for a decision, the fund takes no new voucher.
- **Decision and replenishment (pengisian)**: another manager approves or rejects (a rejection needs a note; the vouchers go back to the custodian). Approved, the difference found at the count is written to the ledger and the fund is replenished by the amount of the fund less the cash counted, so it is back to its full amount. Each step writes audit entries and an outbox event (`finance.petty.voucher_recorded`, `settlement_submitted`, `settlement_approved`, `settlement_rejected`) that carries the expense account's department and category for the management P&L.
- **Closing**: a fund is closed (no new vouchers) only when no voucher waits to be accounted for and no settlement waits for a decision; the cash left in it is returned outside the system.
- Not yet: raising or lowering the amount of a fund, cash returned to the main cash with a ledger entry, a petty cash report by expense account and period (it belongs with the management P&L), and approval of a single large voucher before it is spent.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Finance/PettyCashHttpTest.php` (funds and privileges, vouchers and their limits, idempotency, the ledger floor and immutability triggers, proofs, voiding, settlement with its blockers, approval with a difference, rejection and resubmission, closing a fund).

  - **Goods for resale are costed when consumed.** An invoice under an expense account of the category goods is left out of the invoice costs, because the same goods reach the P&L when the stock is consumed; an invoice not yet classified is left out and shown as a warning with its amount.
  - Warnings: unclassified invoices, unmapped outlets, days not yet verified by finance.
- **Cash flow summary (FR-FIN-031)**: what came in and went out in the range, split between cash and bank (cash is cash; every other method is bank). In: guest payments from the booked days (these already include the company folios settled by finance receipts) and receipts against receivables made by hand. Out: supplier payments that were paid and petty cash vouchers (cash). Transfers between the cashier, finance and petty cash are not movements of the property's cash. The balance at a date is the opening the owner set (cash, bank, opening date, with a reason; every change audited) plus the movements from the opening date to that date.
- **Exports (FR-FIN-034)**: CSV with the byte order mark and safe cells, stable column names, money in major units with two decimals, dates as year-month-day, a range of up to a year and 50,000 rows; each export is written to the audit trail. Data sets: revenue by source, payments received, supplier invoices (payables) with their classification, supplier payments, receivables, receipts and petty cash vouchers.

### Slice 16 (2026-10-03): recurring expenses and department budgets

- Status: `TASK-FIN-016` and `-017` are `REVIEW`. NFR/BR: BR-003 (a settled due date is never changed; a budget change keeps the figures before it in the audit), BR-005 (idempotent), BR-002 (integer money).

### Slice 17 (2026-10-03): corrections to a booked day, reconciliation exceptions and the finance audit trail

- Status: `TASK-FIN-019`, `-035` and `-036` are `REVIEW`; `TASK-FIN-037` stays `IN_PROGRESS` (the payment provider/EDC and bank record are not read yet). NFR/BR: BR-003 (a booked day, a correction, an exception and the audit trail are never edited), BR-004 (a correction is approved, and an exception reconciled, by someone other than the person who raised it), BR-005 (idempotent), BR-002 (integer money), BR-006 (document numbers `COR-nnnnnn` and `EXC-nnnnnn`).
- Context: migration 68 (`fin_corrections`, `fin_correction_lines`, `fin_exceptions`), `CorrectionService`, `ExceptionService`, `FinanceAuditService`, screens `/finance/corrections`, `/finance/corrections/{id}`, `/finance/exceptions` and `/finance/audit`; privileges `finance.correction.approve` (decide corrections; asking for one and raising or reconciling an exception need `finance.reconcile.manage`) and `finance.audit.view`.
- **Corrections (FR-FIN-036).** A booked revenue day, verified or not, never changes. A correction is a journal that points at the day, says why, and has lines: revenue lines (an outlet, with base, service charge and tax apart, signed) and payment lines (a method and a signed amount collected). A different person with the right to approve decides it; approved, it takes effect on the business date of the approval, so a verified day stays as it was verified and the correction is reported in the open period. A rejected correction changes nothing; a wrong correction is corrected by another one. The revenue report shows what the corrections approved in the range add, apart from the booked days; the management P&L and the cash flow include them from their effective date; the exports have a data set for them.
- **Exceptions (FR-FIN-019, FR-FIN-037).** A refund, a chargeback, a settlement discrepancy or a payment of unknown status is kept as an exception dated the business day it belongs to (kind, amount, method, provider or bank reference, the folio or reservation, the description). Someone other than the person who raised it reconciles it: **matched** to the provider or bank record, **adjusted** by a correction (named by its number; a rejected correction does not count) or **waived**, each with the reason. The transaction it belongs to is never edited. A day cannot be verified while an exception of that day is open, in addition to the open cash differences of its shifts; the dashboard warns of the open ones.
- **Audit trail (FR-FIN-035).** The actions that change a financial figure already write audit entries with who, when, the thing, the figures before and after, the reason and the approval. The screen reads them: `finance` (payables, payments, receivables, receipts, petty cash, budgets, corrections, exceptions, exports) or `front_office` (folio postings, cashier shifts, night audits), by range of up to 93 days, person and action, 50 a page, with the figures before and after.
- Not yet: reading the provider or bank record to find the discrepancies by itself (FR-FIN-004), reversal of a supplier payment or a receipt (they need their own reversal documents), corrections to payables and receivables (a credit note and a write-off), and an alert when a day has stayed unverified.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Finance/CorrectionsExceptionsAuditHttpTest.php` (asking for a correction with validation and idempotency, approval by another person and its effective date, the effect on the revenue report, the P&L, the cash flow and the exports, rejection, exceptions with their kinds and reconciliation, the day held by an open exception, the dashboard alert, the audit trail with its filters and privileges, immutability triggers).
