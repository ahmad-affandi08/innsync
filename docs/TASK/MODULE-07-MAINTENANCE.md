## Progress notes

### Slice 29 (2026-10-03): work orders, technicians, photos and rooms off sale

- Status: `TASK-MTC-001` to `-006` are `REVIEW`. ADR/BR: property scope, a closed work order and its history never change, the maintenance context does not touch front office tables (it asks through a contract).
- Context: new bounded context `Maintenance` (`app/Modules/Maintenance`), migration 81 (`maintenance_work_orders`, `maintenance_work_events` append-only by trigger, `maintenance_settings`), `WorkOrderStore` with `DatabaseWorkOrderStore`, `WorkOrderService`, `WorkOrderController`, page `maintenance/pages/work-orders`, routes `/maintenance`. Privileges `maintenance.work.report` (every department), `maintenance.work.perform` (technicians) and `maintenance.work.manage`. In the front office: the `RoomBlocking` contract and `RoomBlockingService`, over `InventoryAdminService::placeBlock` and `removeBlock` (the same rules as a block placed by hand, without the front office's own privilege, which the caller has checked).
- **Report (`MTC-001`).** Anyone with a maintenance privilege reports a fault: a title, details, the kind of work (electrical, plumbing, air conditioning, furniture, appliance, building, IT, other), the department that reports it, where (a room of the property or a named place; one is needed), the priority and an optional photo. It becomes one work order, `WO-000001`. A person who only reports sees only what they reported; technicians see what is given to them; managers see everything.
- **Priority and deadline (`MTC-002`).** Urgent, high, normal, low; each has the time it may take, counted from the report (baseline: one hour, four hours, a day, three days; the manager sets them for the property, in order, audited with the version of the setting). Changing the priority recalculates the deadline from the report. A work order past its deadline is flagged and counted.
- **Technician (`MTC-003`).** The manager gives a work order to a person who holds the privilege to perform; the technician's tab lists their jobs, which works on a phone. A work order is started and worked by the technician it was given to (or a manager); a job nobody holds cannot be started.
- **Status (`MTC-004`).** Open, assigned, in progress, on hold (with what it waits for: parts, a vendor, or access to the room, and a note), done and cancelled (with a reason, by the manager). Every step names the version of the work order that was seen, and is kept in its history with who and when.
- **Proof (`MTC-005`).** Finishing needs a note and a photo of the finished work; without the photo the work order is refused, and the database refuses a done work order with no photo as well. Photos are kept privately, readable only by people who may see work orders, and their retention starts when the work order closes.
- **Rooms (`MTC-006`).** A manager takes the room of a work order off sale as out of order or out of service until a date, from the business date; the front office keeps the block (so no room is sold or overlapped), and says which nights are now oversold so reservations can be moved. The room goes back on sale when the work is done or the work order is cancelled (a block the front office released already is no obstacle), or by hand.
- Not yet: turning a guest request of the maintenance category into a work order, a push notice to the technician's phone.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Maintenance/WorkOrderHttpTest.php` (the report with its number, deadline, photo and checks, who sees what; the whole path from giving the job to the finished photo, holds, stale screens and the history that cannot be changed; priority, service levels and the late flag; a room taken off sale, put back by hand, by completing and by cancelling, with the front office's block). Seen in the browser: a fault reported with a photo, given, started, refused without a photo and finished with one.

### Slice 30 (2026-10-03): escalation by deadline and shift, and the reports

- Status: `TASK-MTC-013` is `REVIEW`; `TASK-MTC-012` was `IN_PROGRESS` until slices 32 and 33 added the cost of parts and of vendor work. ADR/BR: facts that are never edited, audit of every escalation and acknowledgement (by the system, no person), the scheduler and the page do not depend on each other.
- Context: migration 82 (`maintenance_escalations`, `maintenance_room_blocks` as the history of rooms off sale, and the escalation settings on `maintenance_settings`), `EscalationService`, `ReportService`, `ReportController`, command `maintenance:escalate` (scheduled every five minutes), pages `maintenance/pages/reports` and the escalation panel of the work orders page, privilege `maintenance.escalation.receive` for the manager on duty.
- **Escalation (`MTC-013`).** A work order is escalated when it has used a share of its time: the warning share (baseline 75 percent) raises level 1, the whole of it (baseline 100) level 2. The matrix: an urgent or high work order has two levels, a normal or low one only the first; in the day shift level 1 goes to the supervisor (whoever manages work orders) and level 2 to the manager on duty; in the night shift (baseline 22:00 to 06:00 of the property's own day, crossing midnight) both go to the manager on duty. Each level is raised once, kept as a fact with whom it is for and the shift, shown on the page of those people until one of them acknowledges it (with a note, audited), and taken off when the work order closes. The owner sets the two shares and the hours of the night with the service levels. It runs from the scheduler and again when a manager opens the page.
- Not delivered: there is no push, SMS or e-mail to the person yet; escalations are on their screen. A third level (the general manager) and a matrix per outlet are not built.
- **Reports (`MTC-012`).** For a period of up to a year: what was reported, by status and by the department that reported it; the work finished, its average time from the report and the share finished by its deadline, in all and by priority; the rooms with two or more faults and what kind; and the nights each room was off sale because of a work order (counted from the day the room was taken off sale until it went back or its last night, within the period).
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Maintenance/EscalationReportHttpTest.php` (levels by share of time, to whom, once; the matrix by priority and by night shift; acknowledgement, who may and once; closing takes it off; the scheduler without the page; the settings; the reports with the figures above). Seen in the browser: an overdue work order escalating on the page and acknowledged, and the report page.

### Slice 31 (2026-10-03): assets, meter readings and preventive plans

- Status: `TASK-MTC-007`, `-008` and `-014` are `REVIEW`. ADR/BR: an asset is retired, never deleted; meter readings are facts that are never edited (database triggers) and never go down; a preventive work order is made once for each cycle (a unique key in the database), by the system (audit without a person).
- Context: migration 83 (`maintenance_assets`, `maintenance_meter_readings` append-only by trigger, `maintenance_pm_plans`, and `asset_id`, `plan_id`, `pm_due` on `maintenance_work_orders`, unique on `plan_id` + `pm_due`), `AssetStore` with `DatabaseAssetStore`, `AssetService`, `PreventiveService`, `AssetController`, command `maintenance:preventive` (hourly), page `maintenance/pages/assets`, the asset choice on the report form. Privileges stay `maintenance.work.manage` (assets, plans, retiring) and `maintenance.work.perform` (meter readings).
- **Assets (`MTC-007`).** A manager keeps the machines, equipment, vehicles, buildings, IT and other assets of the property: number `AST-000001`, name, kind, serial number, a room or a named place, date acquired, warranty until, notes. The warranty shows as valid, ending (within 60 days) or expired. A retired asset (with a reason and the date) stops its plans. Each asset shows its repairs and care: every work order that names it, with its history; a report may name the asset, and when it names no place it takes the place of the asset.
- **Meter (`MTC-014`).** An asset may have a meter in hours, kilometres or cycles. A technician or manager records a reading; it must not be lower than the last one (the history is kept, with who and when).
- **Preventive plans (`MTC-008`).** A plan by the calendar (every N days, first due on a date or one interval from today, the work order made some days before it falls due) or by the meter (every N units from the reading when the plan was set). The scheduler (and opening the assets page) makes the work order, as an engineering work order with its deadline by priority, once for each cycle; it makes none while the plan has a work order still open. Finishing the work order rolls the plan: the next date counts from the day it was done, the next meter count from the reading then. Cancelling a work order frees its cycle so it may be made again; a plan may be paused and resumed.
- Not yet: depreciation and a link to the finance books, a push notice for preventive work.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Maintenance/AssetPreventiveHttpTest.php` (an asset kept with place, warranty and meter and retired; readings that cannot go down; a calendar plan through its cycle and roll; lead time, cancel, pause and a retired asset; a meter plan falling due; a report naming the asset). Seen in the browser: an asset created with a meter, a reading recorded, the plan form.

### Slice 32 (2026-10-03): spare parts used on a work order and parts requests to purchasing

- Status: `TASK-MTC-009` is `REVIEW`; `TASK-MTC-010` is `IN_PROGRESS` here (requests for parts are done; work done by an outside vendor came with slice 33, which completes it). ADR/BR: the stock is only changed by the inventory (through an event), maintenance never writes inventory tables, the part use is a fact that is never edited (database triggers), purchasing keeps its own rules for the request.
- Context: migration 84 (`maintenance_part_uses` and `maintenance_part_requests`, both append-only by trigger), `PartsStore` with `DatabasePartsStore`, `PartsService`, `PartsController` (routes `/maintenance/work-orders/{id}/parts` and `/part-requests`, both idempotent), the parts panel in the work order dialog, the cost of parts in the reports page. In the inventory: `IngredientCatalog` gained `onHand`, the new contract `PurchaseRequesting` (`PurchaseRequestingService`, over `PurchaseRequestService`) and the consumer `MaintenancePartConsumer` for the event `maintenance.part.used`.
- **Parts used (`MTC-009`).** The technician the work order was given to (or a manager) records, while the work is in progress or on hold, which item of the inventory went into it, from which location and how much, in one of the units of the item. A part that is not on hand at that location is refused, saying what is there; the work order keeps the part with its cost at the moving average of that moment (or "no cost yet" when the item has none), and the history of the work order says it. The inventory takes the stock out by the event, one issue movement for each use (source `maintenance`), so handling the event twice takes nothing twice; the balance may not be blocked by a location or category that forbids going below zero, in which case the message waits like any other. The total parts cost of a work order is shown on it.
- **Parts requests (`MTC-010`).** For a part that is missing, the technician or manager starts a purchase request from the work order: the lines (item, quantity, unit), why, how urgent and the date needed. It is a draft of the person who asks, in the department maintenance, whose reason names the work order; it needs the purchasing privilege to make requests, which purchasing itself checks. The person submits it in purchasing like any request, and it goes for approval by the chain and the budget the owner set. The work order lists its requests with their number and where each stands (draft, waiting for approval, approved, rejected, cancelled, ordered).
- **Report (`MTC-012`).** The reports page adds the cost of the parts used in the period, counted by the business day they were recorded: the total, by kind of work and the five parts that cost the most.
- Not yet: giving an unused part back to stock, asking for equipment that is not an item of the inventory.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Maintenance/PartsHttpTest.php` (a part kept with its cost, the stock taken out once however often the event is handled, the use that cannot be changed; refusals for what is not on hand, a wrong unit, a wrong quantity, someone else's work order, and work not started or already closed; a request that makes a draft in purchasing with its link, which the work order follows after submission, and the refusals for privilege, a past date, no reason, no lines, a bad urgency and a wrong unit; the report's cost of parts). Seen in the browser: a part recorded on a started work order, a request made and listed with its number, and the cost on the report page.

### Slice 33 (2026-10-03): work given to outside vendors

- Status: `TASK-MTC-015` and `TASK-MTC-010` are `REVIEW`; `TASK-MTC-012` is now `REVIEW`: the cost of repairs is the parts and the vendor work (the hours of the staff are not costed). ADR/BR: the approval of the amount goes through the approval gate (subject `maintenance.vendor-job`), quotations are facts that are never edited (database triggers), a job is closed only with its cost and a photo (a CHECK in the database), maintenance reads suppliers only through a purchasing contract.
- Context: migration 85 (`maintenance_vendor_jobs`, `maintenance_vendor_quotes` append-only by trigger), `VendorJobStore` with `DatabaseVendorJobStore`, `VendorJobService`, `VendorJobController` (routes `/maintenance/vendor-work`, `/maintenance/vendor-jobs/...`), page `maintenance/pages/vendor-work`, the vendor cost in the reports page, privilege `maintenance.vendor.manage`. In purchasing: the contract `SupplierDirectory` (`SupplierDirectoryService`). In `config/approvals.php`: the subject `maintenance.vendor-job`, not mandatory.
- **Quotations and choice.** A person with the vendor privilege opens a job on a work order that is not closed (what the vendor is to do), takes up to five quotations of suppliers in use in purchasing (one for each supplier; amount, valid until, a note), and chooses one. When it is not the lowest of several the reason is required and kept. Managers of work orders may see the jobs; only the vendor privilege acts.
- **Approval (`MTC-010`).** The agreed amount goes through the chain the owner configured for its amount band; with no policy for the amount the job is approved at once (baseline). While approvers have not decided nothing goes on; the person who chose takes the decision, as in purchasing: approved, the job goes on; rejected, it is closed.
- **Schedule and finish (`MTC-015`).** An approved job gets the date the vendor comes (from the business date, changeable until it is done). Finishing records what the work really cost, an invoice reference, what was done and a photo of the work (kept privately, with the retention of work order photos); a cost above the agreed amount is flagged on the job and counted in the reports. A job may be cancelled with a reason until it is done.
- **The work order.** Each step of a job is in the history of its work order. A work order is not finished or cancelled while a job of it is open (quoting, waiting for approval, approved or scheduled).
- **Report (`MTC-012`).** The reports page adds the cost of the vendor jobs finished in the period (counted by the day they were done): the total, the number above their quotation, by vendor and by kind of work.
- Not yet: paying the vendor (the invoice goes through purchasing and finance as a supplier invoice; there is no link from the job), a minimum number of quotations as a setting, a validity check of the quotation on the day of choice, closing the approval request when a job is cancelled while it waits (it stays open for the approvers and is never used), vendor contracts and visit reminders.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Maintenance/VendorJobHttpTest.php` (the path from the first quotation to the photo of the work, the work order blocked until then and free after; the reason for a quotation that is not the lowest and a cost above the quotation flagged; an amount over a policy band waiting for the chain, taken by the person who chose, rejected and closed, and a smaller amount needing nobody; who may see and who may act, wrong suppliers, amounts, dates, missing photo or note, stale versions, cancelling, a closed work order, and the quotation that cannot be changed; the cost in the reports). Seen in the browser: a job opened, two quotations taken, the lowest chosen, scheduled and finished with a photo and a cost above the quotation, and the vendor cost on the report page.

### Slice 34 (2026-10-03): routine duties with the steps of their procedure

- Status: `TASK-MTC-011` is `REVIEW`; with it every requirement of the Maintenance module is built to `REVIEW`. ADR/BR: a procedure changes only the duties that fall due after the change (each run keeps the steps it was made with), a finding is never only a tick (it raises a work order), a duty done or missed is a record that the database no longer lets anyone change, nothing is deleted.
- Context: migration 86 (`maintenance_duties`, `maintenance_duty_steps`, `maintenance_duty_runs`, `maintenance_duty_run_steps`, with triggers closing the steps and the run once it is done or missed), `DutyStore` with `DatabaseDutyStore`, `DutyService` (the procedures), `DutyRunService` (the times they fall due), `DutyController`, command `maintenance:duties` (hourly), page `maintenance/pages/duties`. No new privilege: managers write the procedures, technicians and managers answer them.
- **Procedures.** A manager writes a duty: a title, how often (every day, every week on a day of the week, every month on a day from 1 to 28), the shift (day, night or any), the asset it is about or the place, the kind of work, and one to thirty steps (for example the oil level and the fuel tank of the generator, the pressure of the pumps, the filters of the air conditioning). It may be changed with the version of the screen, retired and brought back (from the day it comes back); it is never deleted.
- **Runs.** On each business day the duties that fall due make a run with the steps they have at that moment; this is done by the scheduler and whenever the screen is opened, and a day nobody looked at (up to 31 days) is made up, so what was not done is counted as missed. A run still open when its business day is over is missed and stays on record.
- **Answering.** A technician or manager answers each step fine, issue or not applicable, with a note (the note is required for an issue). An issue raises a work order at once, from engineering, for the asset or the place of the duty, with the step and the note; changing the answer later raises no second one. The run is finished when every step is answered, with an optional note; then neither the run nor its steps change.
- **Report (`MTC-012`).** The reports page adds how the duties went in the period: done, missed, the share done of those that closed, the issues found and, for each duty, how many were done and missed.
- Not yet: more than one run of a duty on a day (a duty of two shifts is two duties), a duty only on some days (weekdays only), a photo or a reading on a step, assigning a run to a person, a push notice before a duty is missed, a different count of days for the catch-up.
- Evidence: `tests/Feature/Maintenance/DutyHttpTest.php` (one run a day with its steps and a change reaching only the next runs; retiring and bringing back; weekly and monthly duties on their day; days nobody looked at made up and missed; steps answered, a finding raising one work order for the asset and place, and a done run that cannot be changed, also by the database; who may see and write, and every refused input; the compliance in the reports). Seen in the browser: a duty written with two steps, its run opened, one step fine and one issue raising a work order, and the duty finished.
