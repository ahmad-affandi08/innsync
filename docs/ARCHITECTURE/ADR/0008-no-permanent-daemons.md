# ADR 0008 — No Permanent Daemons on Shared Hosting

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Do not require Horizon, Reverb, Octane, Supervisor, or self-managed long-running workers in the initial deployment architecture.

## Rationale

Keeps production compatible with the declared Niagahoster/Hostinger shared-hosting target.

## Change rule

A different decision requires a superseding ADR and impact review against PRD/NFRs.
